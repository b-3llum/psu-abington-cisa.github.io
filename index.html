Before you come

Questions students ask us.

Something else? Email mmt6227@psu.edu or message Moussa Toure on Teams.

I’ve never done cybersecurity. Can I still come?

Yes. No prior experience is required. Sessions start from the basics and there is help in the room.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

It helps, but it isn’t required. There are workstations available.

Do I have to be an IT or CS major?

No. Any currently registered Penn State Abington student can join, from any major.

Is hacking legal?

The way we do it, yes. We only practice on systems we own, training platforms, or systems we have permission to test. Responsible skill-building, never unauthorized access.

How do I join?

Join CISA on Penn State Discover to RSVP and get event reminders, then come to the next session.
